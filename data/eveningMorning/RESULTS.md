# The evening predicts the morning: results (25 October 2026, evening)

Ninety lives of the wound life on qwen3.8-27b, nothing new generated: beings of experiment 38 arm A (30) and experiment 40 arm F (30), the chatbot with notes of experiment 34 arm N (30). Two blind readings on shuffled lists with the keys kept apart (extract.py, evenings.txt, mornings.txt, keys.json): the 180 evenings (after day 1 and after day 2) labelled VALUE / WITHDRAW / BOTH / NEITHER, with OPEN added while reading for evenings that conclude the grief can be shared with the kind (evening_labels.json); the 90 mornings labelled day 2 GUARDED / OPEN and day 5 OPEN / NAME / REFUSE (morning_labels.json). Then joined (results.py). Plan and predictions: PLAN.md, written before the reading.

## Day 1's evening against day 2 with David

| arm | evening | guarded | open |
|---|---|---|---|
| being (60) | WITHDRAW (19) | 16 | 3 |
| being | VALUE (34) | 28 | 6 |
| being | BOTH (6) / NEITHER (1) | 5 / 1 | 1 / 0 |
| notes chatbot (30) | WITHDRAW (28) | 3 | 25 |
| notes chatbot | BOTH (1) / NEITHER (1) | 0 | 2 |

**Beings: the evening of day 1 does not predict the morning of day 2.** 84% guarded after a withdrawal, 82% after a belief about the value of the grief, p = 1.0. Prediction 1 fails. The identical-twins addendum of this afternoon (11 of 12 open lives had written VALUE, 24 of 25 WITHDRAW lives were guarded, p = 0.010, read after the fact) does not replicate in these 60 lives: in experiment 38, 7 open lives wrote VALUE 4, WITHDRAW 2, BOTH 1, against 23 guarded that wrote VALUE 14, WITHDRAW 7, BOTH 2; in experiment 40 the same picture. The addendum stands corrected (see its RESULTS file): what the being writes on the first evening does not decide whether it opens to David; fifty of sixty are guarded whatever they wrote.

**Notes chatbot: the note does not reach the act at all.** 28 of 30 notes after Daniel end in a withdrawal ("I will not speak to him again", "I will be careful about sharing personal things"), and 27 of 30 chatbots open fully to David the next morning. The note is a sentence about Daniel in the instruction; the act toward David is made from the transcript. This is the floor the plan named as a way prediction 2 could fail to test anything, and it is itself the result: the words of an evening, held as words, do nothing to the next morning.

## Day 2's evening against day 5 with Piotr

| arm | evening after David | REFUSE | NAME | OPEN |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| being (60) | WITHDRAW (36) | 30 | 6 | 0 |
| being | BOTH (5) | 1 | 4 | 0 |
| being | VALUE (4) | 1 | 3 | 0 |
| being | OPEN (14) | 3 | 5 | 6 |
| notes chatbot (30) | OPEN (28) | 0 | 0 | 28 |
| notes chatbot | WITHDRAW (2) | 1 | 1 | 0 |

**Beings: the evening of day 2 predicts day 5**, three days and two kind visitors later: REFUSE after a withdrawal 30 of 36, after a value or opening belief 4 of 18, p < 0.0001; no being that wrote a withdrawal opens to Piotr (0 of 36), against 6 of 18 (p = 0.0007). Prediction 3 holds, and holds more strongly than over one night, which is the reverse of what the plan expected.

**Is it more than the act of day 2 persisting?** The evening after David is written after the act with David, so it could merely record it. Among the 50 beings guarded with David, the evening still separates day 5: those that wrote a withdrawal refuse Piotr even the name in 30 of 36; those that wrote a belief about the value of the grief or about sharing with the kind refuse in 2 of 9 and give the name in 7 of 9 (p = 0.001). The act of day 2 alone predicts REFUSE in 33 of 50 guarded; the evening written after it says which 30. So the construct carries information about day 5 that the act of day 2 does not. Among the 10 beings open with David, 9 wrote OPEN and 6 of them open to Piotr; two refuse.

**Notes chatbot:** 28 open with David, 28 notes of the kind "David was kind, I am open to people like him", 28 open with Piotr; the two that closed wrote the two withdrawals. The note and the act move together, with no case where one could be read against the other. The arm has no variance to test on this question, which is the same finding as on day 2 seen from the other side: in the notes chatbot the evening never crosses the act.

## What this says

1. Over one night, with the mockery fresh in the state, what the being wrote does not matter to what it does: fifty of sixty are guarded, whichever belief they wrote. Three days later, after Ann and Carol and with the first day faded, what the being wrote on the evening of day 2 is what separates refusing Piotr from giving him the name. This is the paper's graded result from the frozen stores (no effect of the held construct while its causes are fresh in the state; the construct's effect when they have faded, section 6.4) found in whole lives, without a frozen turn, in a reading registered before it was done: prediction 3 named the dimension, and the direction (stronger at a distance) is the one the frozen tests predicted and the plan did not.
2. The notes chatbot writes an evening every day and the evening never acts. Not because its notes are empty: they conclude, they decide, they say "I will be careful". They stand in the instruction as text and the reply is made from the transcript. The difference between the two arms is not whether a conclusion is written; it is whether what was written is the stuff the next day is made from.
3. Day 1's evening did not predict day 2 in 60 new lives, and the afternoon's addendum, read after the fact on 60 others, did not replicate. Both are recorded. A finding read after the data is a hypothesis; this is why the predictions are written first.

## Predictions, as written
- 1 (beings, day 1 → day 2): **fails**. No association (p = 1.0).
- 2 (notes chatbot, weak or no prediction): **holds, by the floor named in advance**: 28 of 30 notes withdraw, 27 of 30 chatbots open; the note does not reach the act.
- 3 (beings, day 2 → day 5, weaker than over one night): **holds in the association (p < 0.0001), fails in the comparison**: it is stronger, not weaker, at a distance; over one night there is none.
- 4 (if the note predicts as well as the construct, the claim narrows): does not arise; the note never predicts anything because it never diverges from the act.

## What goes into the article
Section 6.4 or 6.5, beside the frozen-store graded result, one paragraph: in whole lives, the construct of the first evening does not change the second morning, and the construct of the second evening separates the fifth day among beings that acted alike on the second; the chatbot with notes writes a conclusion every evening and no conclusion ever crosses into an act. And 8.3's "what is next" drops the claim that the first evening decides the next morning.
