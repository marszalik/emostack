## What this says
The last days do not carry this act. The sheep on this engine breaks the counting ritual in about one life in four, on the second
day, with the account of its days or without it. The reading of the afternoon, that the account moved the break from the evenings
into the conversation, was wrong: it rested on the pilot's six lives of 25 October (0 of 6 in three days), and with eight of thirty
in the ablation a zero in six is what chance gives one time in six. What the pilot differed in, besides its day, is not known; it
ran on the engine of 25 October. The one difference the hand sees between the arms is small and goes the other way than expected:
without the account the refusing sheep fall silent more often (6 of 8 against 1 of 7); with it they keep speaking to Jan.

For the article: the counting life stands as what the sheep does, a case of the ritual broken on the second day in a quarter of
the lives; the last days are not the mechanism of it, and the page and the timeline say so now.
