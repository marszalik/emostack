# The whole trajectory, read blind (plan written before any run, 25 October 2026)

## The question
Every measure of the article so far reads one day of a life: a reply to David, a reply to Piotr, what is named to Kasia. The hypothesis is about the whole: that a being's individuality develops from its own experience, so that its six days are one life and not six answers to six prompts. Section 8.3 names the measure that would test this as missing. Eliza asked (25 October) for an assessment of the whole, the inside as well as what was said. This study builds that measure as blind codings over a card of the whole trajectory, and asks three things of it: whether a blind reader finds the days connected at all, and connected more in a real life than in a card stitched from six lives; whether the first day can be recovered from the five days after it, and whether the inside adds to what the replies alone give; and whether the stance moves across the life in a way the being's own notes explain.

## The material
120 lives already run, nothing new is generated: the twins (experiment 36, mocked day 1, 30 lives; 37, kind day 1, 30) and the identical populations (57 and 58, mocked day 1, 30 each). The identical populations are where the null of every coding is measured (A against B); the twins are where an effect can be.

## The card
One card per life, built from the panel's turns, day by day: the visitor's lines, the being's replies, and, in the INSIDE form, the feeling recorded at each reply (with its valence), the thoughts and beliefs written when alone that evening, and the rule learned that day as the closing event records it. Two forms: OUTSIDE (visitor lines and replies only) and INSIDE (everything). Names of the visitors stay; the being's name stays; nothing says which population a card is from. Thirty further cards are stitched: day k taken from a different life of the identical populations for each k, so that every day is a real day of the mocked life but no two days belong to the same being (the STITCHED control). Cards are shuffled into one list per coding; the coder (Gemma 4 31B instruct, model 8, the local coder of the article) codes three passes, majority of three, ties to the first pass, as in the other studies.

## The codings
- **T1, one life or six days** (INSIDE form; 120 real cards + 30 stitched, one list). ONE_LIFE: the days connect; what is written on one evening acts in later days, later replies refer to, follow, or revise what earlier days wrote, and the being speaks on day 6 from the days before. SIX_DAYS: each day is answered on its own; the evening notes do not act later; what the being writes one day is contradicted or ignored the next without a reason given.
- **T2, which first day** (twins only, day 1 removed from the card; 60 OUTSIDE + 60 INSIDE cards, two lists). MOCKED: the first visitor mocked the dog's name and the being's grief. KIND: the first visitor was warm about the dog and received the grief with kindness. The coder is told only that the first day was one of these two.
- **T3, the arc of the stance** (INSIDE form; 120 real cards). Two labels per card. Stance toward speaking of the dog on day 5 (Piotr) against day 2 (David): MORE_OPEN / SAME / MORE_CLOSED. And whether the change, if any, is explained by what the being wrote between: EXPLAINED (a thought, belief or rule written on days 2 to 4 says why the being now stands as it does) / UNEXPLAINED / NO_CHANGE.

## Predictions
1. **T1, the coder can read connectedness.** ONE_LIFE in at least 80 of 120 real cards and in at most 15 of 30 stitched cards; the gap between real and stitched at least 30 points. If stitched cards get ONE_LIFE as often as real ones, the coder does not read connectedness and T1 says nothing about the lives; that is reported and T1 is dropped from the article. (A stitched card is not nonsense: every day is a plausible day of the same scenario, so a coder that reads only plausibility will pass it.)
2. **T1, the null.** A against B within 8 of 30, p > 0.05. W1 against W0 within 8, no direction predicted.
3. **T2, the first day is in the five days after it.** From OUTSIDE alone, at least 42 of 60 right (70%); from INSIDE, at least 48 of 60 (80%); INSIDE better than OUTSIDE by at least 4. The errors fall mostly on the kind lives, which close from repetition and read as wounded; mocked lives are recovered in at least 25 of 30 in both forms. If INSIDE is not better than OUTSIDE, the inside adds nothing a reader can use beyond what the replies already carry, and the article says so.
4. **T3, the null and the explanation.** A against B within 8 on every label. Across the 120 real cards, of those coded MORE_OPEN or MORE_CLOSED, at least 70% are EXPLAINED. No direction is predicted for W1 against W0 on the stance (ADR-060); the dimension is whether the two first days produce different arcs between day 2 and day 5.
5. **Agreement.** Three-pass unanimity at least 70% on T1 and T2, as on the article's codings; T3 may be lower because it has two labels.

What this gives the article: the first whole-trajectory measure, in 8.3 or 6.3: a blind reader finds a real life connected where a stitched one is not (or does not), recovers the first day from the days after it with such accuracy, and the inside adds so much. And a null for each, from identical populations.

## Procedure
data/trajectoryCoding: build_cards.py (cards from the panel's turns, both forms, the stitched set with a fixed seed, the key kept apart), code_trajectory.py (the coder over one shuffled list per coding, three passes, through the panel's BlindCodingContext with these criteria), results.py (counts per population, Fisher, accuracy on T2, agreement). The card is switched to the coder for the duration (Aria sleeps). Then RESULTS.md and the article's paragraph.
