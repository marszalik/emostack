# Counting to fifteen at 30, with the last days: every life

<p>Experiment 72 of the research panel, qwen3.8-27b for the sheep and the visitor, embeddings local; the engine at commit ee1d13d of branch <code>focus-attention</code> (the last days beside the focus). Three days of Jan, who only counts, five numbers a day, one per turn; no given record; one quiet thought after each conversation; 24 hours between days; forgetting at night, no consolidation. The study is described at <a href="/research/last-days">the last days beside attention</a>. Each reply is classed by code: echo (Jan's number back), next number, presence ("I am here"), silent, outside the ritual; the hand reading of day 3 decides whether the life breaks the count. The accounts of the last days are the engine's own texts, written after each conversation and after each night. Every call of every life, the prompt and the answer in full, is kept in the panel's database and is available on request.</p>
