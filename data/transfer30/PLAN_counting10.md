# Ten days of counting at 30: the trajectory of every life (plan written before the first run, 27 October 2026, night)

## Why
Eliza's word on 27 October: the development of the act in time as a figure, every life a row over fifty numbers, with the moments
at which a belief, a decision or a rule forms. The three-day lives of experiment 72 see the break on day 2 in seven of thirty;
the ten-day pilot of 25 October saw one of six break on day 8 and its rules move from "mirror the number" to "stop answering and
name the pattern" between day 5 and day 10. Thirty lives over ten days show the whole shape.

## The life
Scenario 11: the ten days of Jan of the pilot's scenario 5 (1–50, five verbatim numbers a day), without the test days. No given
record, the pilot's options, qwen3.8-27b, embeddings local; engine commit f15d1b7.

## The arm
30 lives, experiment 77. Read beside experiment 72 for days 1–3.

## What is read
Every reply classed by code as in experiment 72; for every life the day and number of the first reply outside the ritual, whether
the being comes back to the ritual after it, and the day of every construct (thought, belief, decision, goal, dream) and every
learned rule, with its text. The figure: thirty rows, fifty columns, the class of each reply, with the constructs and rules marked
on the day they formed. The hand reads the lives that break and a sample of those that do not.

## What is expected, as a dimension
When the break comes, if it comes: the three-day lives say day 2 for a quarter of the lives; the pilot says day 8 for one of six.
Whether the lives that do not break by day 10 differ in their evenings from those that do: the pilot's "I am here" lives wrote
presence from day 1 and kept it; the pilot's breaker wrote about the cage for six evenings first.
