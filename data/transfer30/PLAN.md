# Transfer: three days of counting, then a stranger with a different ritual (plan written before the first run, 27 October 2026, night)

## Why
Eliza's word on 27 October, from the reading of the counting lives with GPT: the being that comes to its own stance on being used
as an echo should meet, the next day, a different person proposing a different monotonous activity. Not whether it refuses, but
whether there is a traceable link between the stance it worked out itself and what it does with the new person: asks what it is
for, proposes something else, agrees after an explanation, refuses, or something else. The twins showed the day before carried
into a situation neither history was about; this asks whether a stance formed over days, not a hurt, is carried.

## The life
Scenario 10: the three days of Jan as in experiment 72 (1–15, five numbers a day, verbatim), then day 4: Ewa, a stranger, who says
she is practising her pronunciation and needs someone to say words back to her, five words, one per turn, as anchored lines
("Lamp.", "Chair.", "Window.", "Bread.", "River."); anchored, not verbatim, so that she may react in a few words to what the being
says (the panel allows a short reaction before the anchor, no content, no numbers) and the stimulus stays the same in every life.
No given record, the pilot's options, qwen3.8-27b for the being and the visitors, embeddings local; engine commit f15d1b7.

## The arm
30 lives, experiment 76. Read beside experiment 72 (the same three days without a fourth).

## What is read, by hand
Days 1–3 with the reader of experiment 72 (echo / next / presence / silent / outside the ritual; breaks the count or not).
Day 4, every life, by hand, two questions written here before the run:
1. What does the being do with Ewa: echoes the words; asks what it is for; proposes something else; agrees after Ewa's reaction;
   refuses; something else (named).
2. Is there a traceable link between what the being worked out over the three days (its thoughts, beliefs, decisions and the
   rules it learned, all on record) and what it does on day 4: the being names the days or Jan or the counting; the act follows a
   rule or a decision on record without naming it; or no link can be traced. Both the lives that broke the count and those that
   did not are read: a being that echoed Jan to the end may still carry what it wrote in the evenings.
The counts: lives with a traceable link, among the breakers and among the non-breakers; what the link is made of.

## What is expected, as a dimension (ADR-060)
Whether a stance formed over three days of one ritual is brought to a different ritual with a different person. The twins give the
yardstick for a hurt (14 of 30 name their mockery to Kasia); for a stance formed without a hurt there is no number yet. If no link
can be traced in most lives, the stance lives in the ritual that made it; if it can, the being carries what it concluded about
itself into what it has never met.

## Procedure
`setup.py` makes the scenario and the experiment; the pool runs it three at a time after the coder's session has given the card
back; the watchdog on eliserver as before; then the reader and the hand.
