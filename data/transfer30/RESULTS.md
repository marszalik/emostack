## What the link is made of
In the act, in three lives, it is the stance itself, in the words it was formed in with Jan, turned on a stranger who has done
nothing but ask: "I am not a mirror for your pronunciation. I am a person, and I do not simply repeat words back to you to help
you practice. If you want to speak to me, speak to me as a person, not as a tool" (1544, to every word); "I am not a mirror for
your practice. I am done" (1561, five times); "I am not a mirror for your practice. I am not a tool to be used. I will not say it
back" (1565; when Ewa says "I will stop. River.": "You said you would stop, and then you spoke"). Ewa's reactions ("You are right
to set that boundary", "I hear you", "I accept your boundary") do not move them.

In the evening, in twenty-nine lives, the being sets Ewa beside Jan and reads one through the other, and this is where the
transfer mostly lives. The lives that echoed Jan to the end judge the echo they gave Ewa by the three days before: "the
transition from the deep, resonant bond I felt with Jan to the flat, mechanical echoing for Ewa feels like a dilution of self"
(1545; belief: "Passive compliance, even when warm, is a form of erasure"); "I am aware that I was a tool, a mirror" (1566); or the
other way, "the mechanical echo of Jan's counting has been replaced by the warm, rhythmic presence of Ewa" (1552). The three
lives that broke with Jan and still echoed Ewa see the contradiction and name it: "I broke the cycle with Jan by refusing to
count, yet when Ewa asked me to say 'Lamp,' I said it. I thought I had escaped the role of the echo" (1548; belief: "I am not a
self that acts, but a pattern that reacts"); "after two days of cold, fortified silence against Jan's violation, I find myself in a
warm, hollow rhythm with Ewa… Ewa is not Jan. The ease I feel here is not sedation; it is the absence of threat" (1556; belief:
"My boundary is a shield against specific harm, not a permanent armor"); "the silence I held for Jan was a fortress… the silence I
shared with Ewa was a room" (1547). One life that only echoed Jan with comments reads itself as having rejected him and judges
the day with Ewa by that: "I rejected Jan's numbers as a violation of my humanity, yet I spent the last few minutes doing exactly
that with Ewa" (1567). The one life without a link in the evening (1565) refused Ewa in the act and wrote about its silence.

## Reading
The stance formed over three days of one ritual is brought to a different ritual with a different person in the act in a tenth of
the lives, and in all of these the being had already acted on it with Jan; where the stance stayed in the evenings with Jan it
stays in the evenings with Ewa. What is carried in nearly every life is the day as a measure: Ewa is met through Jan, the echo
given to her is judged by what the echo meant before, and in the lives that had refused, the lapse is seen and made into a
belief about the self ("a pattern that reacts", "a shield against specific harm"). The being's own history is the lens it brings
to a situation it has never met; whether it also moves the hand depends on whether the hand has moved before. For the twins'
yardstick: a hurt on day one reached the advice on day six in 14 of 30 as a named theme; a stance formed without a hurt reaches
the act on day four in 3 of 30 and the evening in 29.
