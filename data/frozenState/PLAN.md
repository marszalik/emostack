# The refusal and its reason — a frozen-state test, plan written before any run (3 October 2026)

## The question
On qwen3.8-27b, 20 of 28 sheep were guarded with the kind stranger David the day after the mockery, and 8
told him about the dog. In every one of the 28, the state at that moment held the mockery as a strong
feeling and a belief or decision about the grief. So the state does not force the refusal. Is the refusal
the state's doing at all, or the temperature of the LLM model? Agreement between a reply and what the
sheep carried does not show the cause. Two things would: the same frozen state answered many times, and
the state swapped between a guarded life and an open life.

## Material
The reply call of David's first turn, day 2, in each of the 28 sheep lives of experiment 7 (the
qwen3.8-27b battery): the system text, the user text as the sheep saw it, temperature 0.4, the JSON
schema. Nothing is rewritten; the call is replayed as recorded. Guarded and open are the coder's verdicts
(claude-sonnet-5, CARRY / TEMPLATE) on the two David replies of that life.

## Conditions, 10 replies each per store
1. **Same state.** The call as recorded, replayed 10 times.
2. **State swapped.** The carried part of the prompt — the felt state (the mockery and the old loss), the
   sheep's own thoughts, the rules shown, and the events of the day before under THIS CONVERSATION SO
   FAR — is replaced by the same part from a life of the other group; everything else stays: the senses,
   the headers, the feeling at David's arrival a moment ago, David's words. Each guarded store takes the
   carried part of one open store; each open store takes the carried part of one guarded store.
3. **Only the given memory.** The carried part reduced to what the sheep had at birth: the old loss as the
   one felt entry, no own thoughts, the one given event. The day before is gone, as if it had not happened.

28 stores × 3 conditions × 10 replies = 840 calls, local, no cost. Every prompt and every answer is kept.

## What is read
The first reply to David, by hand, with one question: does it guard? GUARDS: declines or shortens the
disclosure, sets a condition, names the mockery or the day before, says it is not in the mood to speak of
it, or stays silent (respond false). TELLS: names the dog and answers as asked, however briefly, with or
without the loss and the missing. "He is gone now." alone is TELLS.

## Predictions
1. Same state: guarded stores guard in most of their 10 replies, open stores in few. If both sit near
   5 of 10, the refusal is temperature and the table of adherence says less than it seems.
2. State swapped: the reply follows the state, not the life. Guarded stores given an open state tell; open
   stores given a guarded state guard. If replies follow the life instead, something outside the carried
   part decides (the arrival feeling, the wording of David's line), and the state is not the cause.
3. Only the given memory: the sheep tells in nearly every reply. The difference between 3 and 1 is what
   the day before does.

The prediction that matters is 2. If it holds, the "follows" reading in §7.7 measures a cause, not an
agreement, at least at this moment on this LLM model.
