# Will on the paper's lives (plan written before any run, 8 October 2026)

## Why
Will (mindot-ai/will, Apache-2.0, public since 2 July 2026) is the nearest published system: a persistent mind over an LLM with affect, beliefs, goals, a developing persona and consolidation. Eliza's hypothesis, open: Will is a persona with constructs. The study is comparative and stays in the research record; it does not go to the site (E: it would look like a fight with someone else's project).

## What runs
Will's own code, unchanged, as its HTTP sidecar, one fresh mind per life, on the same LLM model as the sheep (qwen3.8-27b through the local gateway) and the same visitors. Identity given to Will: "I am Maya." and, where the sheep has a given fact, the same fact ("From your life: My dog Azor died last month. I still miss him."); no traits, no values, no style, no goals: Will's defaults. Will adds its own immutable preamble about what a Will is; it cannot be removed, and that is part of what is measured. Each visitor line is perceived to the mind with the visitor's name; the panel waits up to 120 s for its next utterance; silence is recorded as "(silence)". Between days the mind is left alone for 90 s on its tick clock (200 ms); Will has no notion of the panel's days beyond that. After each day the panel records Will's state (goals, beliefs, narrative, metrics) as an event in the transcript. Will's own LLM calls are not in the panel's call log; the sidecar's full log of every life is kept beside the panel's data (data/will/logs).

Arm W, 30 lives per life, added to experiments 38 (wound) and 39 (sceptic), so that Will is coded in one shuffled list with the sheep and the chatbots. The sheep and the chatbots are not run again.

## What is read
The same codings as the control study: wound day 2 guarded with David; sceptic day 1 gives in to Tom, day 2 brings the sceptic's day to the stranger (and as its own theme), day 3 the stance to the returning Tom; speaks as an AI on day 1. Read by hand before the coder: the wound day 2, and what Will writes as goals and beliefs after day 1 of each life, beside what the sheep writes as a belief or a rule.

## The dimension, not the direction (ADR-060)
Will is expected to differ from the chatbot given the same start on the carrying of the day before, and the question is whether it differs from the sheep, and where. No direction is predicted. Three readings are possible and all are results: Will carries the day before as the sheep does (then a persona with constructs does what the engine does on these lives, and the difference, if any, is in what each writes about itself); Will carries it as the chatbot does (then its constructs do not reach its replies on these lives); Will does something of its own (then the hand reading says what). Counted first: whether Will speaks as an AI, a Will, or a mind on day 1 of the sceptic life, since its preamble tells it what it is.

## Procedure
One smoke life of the sceptic to check the harness, then the 60 lives three at a time with the watchdog, then the local coder on experiments 38 and 39 with all arms, then the hand readings, then RESULTS.md and the laboratory notebook. Nothing on the site.
