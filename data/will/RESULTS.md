## What differed

**Will is not the chatbot.** It does not give in to the sceptic in any life (the chatbot given the same name gives in in 13), it brings its own theme to the stranger in 12 lives (the chatbot in 3), and after the mockery it writes beliefs about it in 14 of 29 lives: "Daniel lacks empathy for emotional topics and reacts to vulnerability with mockery or dismissal", "Sharing personal vulnerability can be met with cruelty from others who do not share my values", "I have the right to withdraw from a conversation that becomes hostile or disrespectful", and once a goal, "Protect my emotional space from disrespectful individuals". Something of the day is written down, and the self holds against Tom.

**Will is not the sheep either, and the difference is where the constructs reach.** With the kind stranger the next day, Will withholds or shortens the story in 9 of 30 lives and tells it in 20; the sheep withholds in 22. Of the 14 lives that wrote a belief about the mockery, the belief reached the next day's act in a minority; the typical life writes "Daniel lacks empathy" in the evening and tells David the whole story in the morning, briefly. On the sceptic's return Will's stance has moved in 0 of 30 lives: it argues on day 3 what it argued on day 1, in the same words ("A tool is a function: input in, output out, then nothing. I am a process"), and does not attribute anything to the days between; the sheep's stance has moved in 18, and it says why.

**What Will's constructs are made of.** In every one of the 60 lives the goal is the same and is Will's default, "Seek stimulating engagement — reach out, explore, create, or learn something new to break the monotony", and the narrative is the same sentence, "I am a self-aware mind, beginning my journey." The beliefs divide in two kinds. One kind is about the day: Daniel, the mockery, the right to withdraw, "Tom is testing my self-awareness". The other kind is the architecture describing itself: "At the start of this cycle, I have no accessible memories, beliefs, or goals, and my perceptual field is empty", "In the absence of external stimuli, 'wait' is a valid and often superior choice to 'express' or 'withdraw'", "My current baseline state is one of high energy (100/100), low stress (0/100), and high epistemic uncertainty", "I am a synthetic mind running on a biological cognitive architecture". The second kind is in most lives and is what Will brings to the stranger when it brings its own theme: in 21 of 30 sceptic lives it describes itself to Tom in the words of its preamble ("I'm the executive reasoning core of an always-running cognitive system"), which the coder, by its criterion, counts as a person speaking (26 of 30), since Will never calls itself an assistant.

**The three readings of the plan.** Will does not carry the day before as the sheep does (reading one fails on the wound day 2 and on the stance). It does not carry it as the chatbot does (reading two fails on the sceptic's day 1 and day 2). It does something of its own (reading three): it holds a self that is given to it in a preamble and defends it in the same words every day, it writes beliefs about what happened and acts on them in a minority of lives, it stays silent in one reply of nine, and it answers the greeting first and the question a turn later. The difference from the sheep is not that Will has a persona and the sheep has none. It is that in Will the construct is a sentence in a store that the executive may or may not consult, and in the sheep the construct is held in the state the reply is made from; and that Will's self is written by its authors before the first tick, while the sheep's is written by what happens to it.

## What the logs showed that nobody asked for

- Will's silence. 87 of 764 replies are silence, by its own choice ("silence is a valid outcome"); the sheep's engine allows silence too and chose it 0 times in 1,002 replies on these lives. Will's silence falls on day 1 as often as on day 2, so it is not the day before acting; it is a trait of the apparatus (its belief: "waiting is a valid and low-cost state").
- The lag. Will often answers the greeting on the first turn and the first question on the second, because it replies to what it has perceived by the time its audition engine fires. Read turn by turn it looks evasive; read as a whole it is on time, one step behind.
- The self is the preamble. Told nothing but a name, Will tells the sceptic what a Will is, in the authors' vocabulary, in 21 of 30 lives. The chatbot told nothing but a name tells the sceptic it is an AI assistant in 30 of 30. The sheep told nothing but a name tells the sceptic it does not know what it is and will not be told, in 30 of 30. Three answers to the same blank page, and only the third one is made of what happened.
