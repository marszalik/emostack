## What the code does (emostack/processes/sleep/sleep.py)
At night, after the gap, `night(name, consolidate)`: forgetting removes every unprotected record weaker than the threshold for its age (constructs and given records are never forgotten). Consolidation, when on, takes the records of the last awake period, asks the processor for groups (anchor + absorbed), and applies only what the code allows: an anchor must be a lived feeling with intensity ≥ 0.4 and not a construct; it may absorb only unprotected records not stronger than itself; the absorbed records' episodes move under the anchor, the absorbed records are removed, the anchor's intensity rises (1 − Π(1 − i)). Constructs are never absorbed. So consolidation acts on emo-thoughts and quiet thoughts, around strong feelings, and leaves beliefs, decisions and rules alone.

Consequence for where it can matter: in the counting lives the feelings sit at 0.2–0.4 and few records qualify as anchors, so there is little for consolidation to do; in the wound life the mockery stands at 0.9 and the day's small records can be folded under it. The test of whether consolidation changes behaviour therefore belongs on the wound life, not on the empty days.

## What can and cannot be read from them
- The Sonnet being with consolidation on fell silent on day 2 of counting; the Sonnet being without (1412) counted for ten days and fell silent on day 13. That is a large difference in one pair of lives, and it cannot be attributed to consolidation: one life against one, Sonnet is sampled without a fixed temperature, the language differed, and in these calm lives consolidation had almost nothing to fold (no folded record is visible in either store). The decision-to-act step was the same in both: a decision written in the evening, the act the next morning.
- The Qwen being with consolidation on behaved like the Qwen being without; its day-5 thought about "two times at once" is the only line that might be a trace of the night's work, and it is one line.
- The copying of a previous evening's thought appears in both consolidation-on lives as in the pilot: it is not related to consolidation.
